Dub is a big part of the music I collect, play and produce, and it has a big influence on the plugins I build, like [dub_siren](/projects/dub-siren) and [flux_filter](/projects/flux-filter). These are the five records from this year that I can't get enough of.

## Priori – 9

<Album
  player="1250906586"
  title="Priori – 9"
  label="Kynant Records"
  released="2026-03-13"
  url="https://kynantrecords.bandcamp.com/album/priori-9"
>

This album sits right between house and techno, and the dubby sounds give it a lot of depth and atmosphere. It's hypnotic and groovy but never rushed, with a very warm low end. I love the vocals and bassline on A1.

</Album>

## K Wata – Give U Space

<Album
  player="1450821438"
  title="K Wata – Give U Space"
  label="Short Span"
  released="2026-05-15"
  url="https://kwata.bandcamp.com/album/give-u-space"
>

Deep atmospheres that sound like nothing else. Different textures are layered on top of each other, yet it stays minimal. There's a dubby low end, subtle grooves and a lot of detail to discover. Listening to it feels like exploring unpredictable landscapes of deep sound.

</Album>

## Sato – Blue, Black And Grey

<Album
  player="1250197705"
  title="Sato – Blue, Black And Grey"
  label="Chapelle XIV Music"
  released="2026-09-10"
  url="https://satoshitomiie.bandcamp.com/album/blue-black-and-grey-2"
>

The classic analog dub techno sound: slowly evolving tracks, groovy basslines and melting dub chords. Over eight tracks it moves smoothly from ambient through techno to house.

</Album>

## JANKA – Exit The Dream LP

<Album
  player="803129618"
  title="JANKA – Exit The Dream LP"
  label="Moonshine Recordings"
  released="2026-09-18"
  url="https://moonshinerecordings.bandcamp.com/album/exit-the-dream-lp"
>

Bass-heavy and atmospheric at the same time. It uses classic dub sounds but is very versatile, with influences from jungle, ambient and dubstep. The moods range from hazy atmospheres to fast, percussive breakbeats.

</Album>

## Loidis – From The Toolkit Of Lovers

<Album
  player="2355311071"
  title="Loidis – From The Toolkit Of Lovers"
  label="Incienso"
  released="2026-10-23"
  url="https://huercosplonk.bandcamp.com/album/from-the-toolkit-of-lovers"
>

A very long album: twelve tracks and almost two hours of soft minimal house with a lot of dub influence. I can listen to every track without getting bored. It's very well produced, rhythmic and positive, good for relaxing and for dancing.

</Album>
